## Scent and memory research

- Herz, R. S. (1997). [The effects of cue distinctiveness on odor-based context-dependent memory](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9184489/). *Memory & Cognition*, 25(3), 375-380. DOI: `10.3758/BF03211293`.
  - Corrects the journal name shown in the submission PDF.

- Sorokowska, A., Nord, M., Stefanczyk, M. M., & Larsson, M. (2022). [Odor-based context-dependent memory: influence of olfactory cues on declarative and nondeclarative memory indices](https://doi.org/10.1101/lm.053562.121). *Learning & Memory*, 29(5), 136-141.

These studies support a limited claim: reinstating an odor context can assist recall in some memory tasks. They do not establish that a scent reproduces a meditation state, improves focus, treats stress, or delivers a therapeutic outcome. Those remain product hypotheses requiring ethical, consent-based testing.

## Product, fragrance, and data guardrails

- [U.S. FDA: Registration & Listing of Cosmetic Product Facilities and Products](https://www.fda.gov/cosmetics/registration-listing-cosmetic-product-facilities-and-products).
  - Reference point for MoCRA facility-registration and cosmetic-product-listing requirements; exemptions and product-specific obligations require specialist review.

- [European Commission: Regulation (EC) No 1223/2009 on cosmetic products](https://health.ec.europa.eu/publications/regulation-ec-no-12232009_en).
  - Reference point for any future skin-contact format intended for the EU market.

- [European Commission: GDPR data minimisation and storage limitation](https://commission.europa.eu/law/law-topic/data-protection/rules-business-and-organisations/principles-gdpr/overview-principles/what-data-can-we-process-and-under-which-conditions_en).
  - Supports the proposed Agent Memory principles: collect only necessary data, provide clear purpose and control, and retain it no longer than necessary.

- [European Data Protection Board: Basic GDPR principles](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/topics/key-gdpr-concepts/basic-principles_en).

- [International Fragrance Association: IFRA Standards](https://ifrafragrance.org/initiatives-positions/safe-use-fragrance-science/ifra-standards).
  - IFRA Standards can prohibit, restrict, or set purity criteria for fragrance materials. They do not replace applicable law, a finished-product safety assessment, or category-specific regulatory review.
