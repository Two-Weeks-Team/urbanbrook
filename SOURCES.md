## Scent and memory research

- Herz, R. S. (1997). [The effects of cue distinctiveness on odor-based context-dependent memory](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9184489/). *Memory & Cognition*, 25(3), 375-380. DOI: `10.3758/BF03211293`.

- Sorokowska, A., Nord, M., Stefańczyk, M. M., & Larsson, M. (2022). [Odor-based context-dependent memory: influence of olfactory cues on declarative and nondeclarative memory indices](https://doi.org/10.1101/lm.053562.121). *Learning & Memory*, 29, 136-141.

These studies support a limited claim: reinstating an odor context can assist recall in some memory tasks. They do not establish that a scent reproduces a meditation state or delivers a therapeutic outcome. That remains a product hypothesis requiring ethical, consent-based testing.
